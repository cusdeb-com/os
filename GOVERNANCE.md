# CusDeb OS Governance

## Core Team

The Core Team is responsible for the technical direction, architecture, and development of CusDeb OS.

Core Team members participate in technical decision-making, review major proposals, and maintain the project's long-term vision.

### Members

- [Evgeny Golyshev](https://github.com/eugulixes) (Project Lead)
- [Artem Lavrukhin](https://github.com/Artem4590)

## Project Lead

The Project Lead is a member of the Core Team responsible for the overall direction of CusDeb OS.

Technical decisions are made collaboratively whenever possible. In cases of unresolved disagreements, the Project Lead has final decision-making authority.
